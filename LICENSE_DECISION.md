| License | What it would mean |
| --- | --- |
| Apache-2.0 | Permissive, explicit patent grant, attribution required |
| MIT | Permissive, short, no patent grant |
| BUSL / source-available | Use allowed under conditions; production SaaS often restricted |
| Proprietary / all rights reserved | No grant; contact the owner |

text of that license and delete this document, or keep this document as history
